How it works

  1. In Google Calendar on a computer: Settings → Import & export → Export. You get a .zip with one .ics per calendar.
  2. Unzip it and pick the calendar's .ics file above.
  3. Group by exact title, or by the text before a colon (e.g. Filming: Client → Filming).
Free, runs entirely in your browser. Expands weekly/daily/monthly repeats and skips cancelled or deleted occurrences. Times use each event's own clock time. AI-assisted build.
